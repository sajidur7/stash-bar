Installed in a minute.

Stashbar is an independent app, so macOS asks you to confirm it once.

01

Download & drag

Open Stashbar.dmg and drag Stashbar into Applications.

02

Allow it once

Open Stashbar. If macOS says it can’t verify the developer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

03

Or use Terminal

One command clears the quarantine flag:

xattr -cr /Applications/Stashbar.app

Good questions.

Download .dmg

Is it free?

Yes. Download, drag to Applications, done.

Do you read my clipboard?

Only to spot URLs, and only on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded unless you stash it.

What if I delete the app?

Guests lose their links. Signed-in users get them all back.

Which Macs?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Why does macOS warn me?

Stashbar isn’t notarised by Apple yet. See Install for the one-time fix.

Can I export my links?

Anytime — JSON, Markdown or browser bookmarks, from Settings → Data.
