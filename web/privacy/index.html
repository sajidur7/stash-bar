Your links are yours.

01

What we store

When signed in: the links you stash (URL, title, tags, notes, pinned/archived state), your email and name from Apple or Google, your avatar if you upload one, and the names of the Macs you use Stashbar on. As a guest, nothing leaves your Mac.

02

Your clipboard

Stashbar checks the clipboard for web addresses on your Mac only. Nothing you copy is uploaded unless you choose to stash it. To show a page title, Stashbar fetches the page you copied, directly from your Mac.

03

Sign in

Sign-in is handled by Apple or Google through our backend provider (Supabase). We never see your password. Your data is stored with row-level security, so only your account can read your links.

04

Analytics

This version of Stashbar collects no analytics and no crash reports. This website uses no trackers or cookies.

05

Deleting your data

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account, links, profile and devices from our servers immediately, and from your other Macs the next time they sync.
